# Portrait credits

Demo portraits for the SNK Courier console prototype. All from Unsplash under
the [Unsplash Licence](https://unsplash.com/license) — free to use, no
attribution required, but recorded here so the provenance is traceable.

Filenames match the `id` in `DRIVERS` / `PARTNERS` / `ACTIVITY` in
`lib/ops/data.ts`; the lookup lives in `lib/ops/people.ts`. `abc.jpg` is the
bookkeeper who logs the entries in the client's own activity feed.

**Before this ships:** `syed.jpg` and `kiani.jpg` stand in for two real people
— the client's own partners. Swap them for their actual photographs, or drop
back to initials for those two. A stock face on a named colleague is worse
than no face.

Source: images.unsplash.com, 192x192, face-cropped.
